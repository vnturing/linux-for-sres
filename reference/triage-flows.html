The troubleshooting loop

Lesson 1.

flowchart LR
  S["Symptom: what, since when, who is affected"] --> H["Rank two or three hypotheses"]
  H --> C["Run the cheapest command that tells them apart"]
  C --> Dq{"Did it rule something in or out?"}
  Dq -->|yes| N["Narrow the list, or mitigate"]
  Dq -->|no| H
  N --> H

USE: pick a resource

Lesson 2.

flowchart TD
  P["Symptom"] --> R["Pick a resource: CPU, memory, disk, network"]
  R --> E{"Errors?"}
  E -->|yes| X["Fault found: chase the error source"]
  E -->|no| S{"Saturated?"}
  S -->|yes| Y["Bottleneck suspect: find who is queued"]
  S -->|no| U{"Utilization high?"}
  U -->|yes| Z["Near the limit: watch for saturation"]
  U -->|no| O["Resource exonerated: next resource"]
  O --> R

Load is high

Lesson 4.

flowchart TD
  L["Load average high versus nproc"] --> V["vmstat 1: compare r and b"]
  V --> R{"r above the CPU count?"}
  R -->|yes| C["CPU saturated: pidstat 1 shows who"]
  R -->|no| B{"b high or iowait high?"}
  B -->|yes| D["Tasks stuck in I/O: check disk and NFS"]
  B -->|no| S{"steal high on a VM?"}
  S -->|yes| N["Hypervisor is taking CPU: resize or move"]
  S -->|no| W["Load was brief: check history with sar -q"]

Memory model

Lesson 5.

flowchart LR
  RAM["Total RAM"] --> A["Anonymous memory: heaps and stacks, freed only by swapping or killing"]
  RAM --> PC["Page cache: file data, reclaimable"]
  RAM --> F["Free: unused"]
  PC --> AV["Available is roughly free plus reclaimable cache"]
  F --> AV

No space left on device

Lesson 6.

flowchart TD
  W["No space left on device"] --> Dq{"df -h shows 100 percent?"}
  Dq -->|no| I{"df -i shows 100 percent inodes?"}
  I -->|yes| I2["Too many small files: find the directory"]
  I -->|no| Q["Check quotas, a read-only remount, or a different mount"]
  Dq -->|yes| U{"Does du -xh find the space?"}
  U -->|yes| T["Rotate or trim the big files"]
  U -->|no| O["lsof +L1: deleted files still held open"]

Client cannot reach service

Lesson 7.

flowchart TD
  C["Client cannot reach service"] --> N{"Does the name resolve?"}
  N -->|no| DNS["DNS or resolver: getent hosts, dig, resolv.conf"]
  N -->|yes| K{"What does connect do?"}
  K -->|"refused, instantly"| R["Host answered, nothing accepted: ss -tlnp, bind address, REJECT rule"]
  K -->|"hangs, then times out"| TO["Packets vanish: firewall DROP, route, security group, host down"]
  K -->|"connects but slow"| SL["Loss, retransmits, DNS delays, busy app: nstat, sar -n TCP,ETCP, tcpdump"]

Service unit life cycle

Lesson 8.

stateDiagram-v2
  [*] --> inactive
  inactive --> activating: start
  activating --> active: ExecStart running
  activating --> failed: exits or times out
  active --> failed: crash or killed
  failed --> activating: auto-restart
  failed --> inactive: stop or reset-failed

Service is up but stuck

Lesson 9.

flowchart TD
  H["Service is up but stuck or wrong"] --> S["ps -o stat,wchan -p PID"]
  S --> Dq{"State D?"}
  Dq -->|yes| K["Waiting in the kernel on I/O: iostat, dmesg, NFS"]
  Dq -->|no| C{"Using CPU?"}
  C -->|yes| P["Hot loop: perf top or strace -c"]
  C -->|no| W["Blocked in a syscall: strace -f -p PID shows which"]

Log pipeline shape

Lesson 10.

flowchart LR

  B --> C["Group: sort"]
  C --> Dq["Count: uniq -c"]

Incident order of operations

Lesson 11.

flowchart TD
  A["Alert or report"] --> B["Capture about 60 seconds of evidence"]
  B --> C{"Is there a recent change to undo?"}
  C -->|yes| Dq["Roll back"]
  C -->|no| E["Shed load, drain, or scale"]
  Dq --> F{"Recovered?"}
  E --> F
  F -->|no| G["Restart only after evidence is saved"]
  F -->|yes| H["Diagnose from the evidence, fix, write it down"]
  G --> F
